🎯 Accuracy Settings

Your compass angle may not be perfectly measured. Enter how far you think each reading could be off. The calculator will then estimate a likely coordinate range instead of pretending the result is guaranteed.

Example: 1° means a reading of 30° is treated as approximately 29°–31°.

ℹ️ How to use it

  1. Put a lodestone at a known location.
  2. Look at the compass and determine the direction/angle it points.
  3. Enter that lodestone's X and Z coordinates.
  4. Enter the compass angle.
  5. Repeat from another location.
  6. Use at least two readings that point in different directions.
  7. Set your estimated angle uncertainty.
  8. Press Calculate Base.

For better accuracy, use 3–5 readings from different locations.

Important: the mathematical intersection can be exact for the numbers entered, but the real Minecraft base position is not guaranteed because your compass angle and lodestone coordinates may contain errors.
