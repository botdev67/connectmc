ℹ️ How It Works

Minecraft uses an approximate 8:1 coordinate ratio between the Overworld and Nether.

Overworld → Nether:
X ÷ 8 and Z ÷ 8

Nether → Overworld:
X × 8 and Z × 8

Y is not converted because Nether and Overworld Y coordinates do not use the same scaling rule.
